# Code of conduct

Be direct, respectful, and focused on improving the software.

- Critique behavior, code, evidence, and design decisions—not contributors.
- Assume good intent while still requiring reproducible evidence.
- Do not publish private file contents, credentials, access tokens, personal information, or vulnerability details in public issues.
- Disclose AI assistance when it materially generated or transformed a contribution.
- Do not submit output that you cannot explain, test, or maintain.
- Accept correction when tests or runtime evidence contradict an assumption.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or users.
